Our first steps together

Small acts,
lasting joy.

Here are the first two projects Carebridge has completed so far. We’re grateful to everyone who helped make them possible.

Project 01

Panti Asuhan Mutiara Mulia

For our first project, Carebridge members visited Panti Asuhan Mutiara Mulia with monetary aid and plenty of energy to share.

We spent time together through activities like senam and singing. The visit was a joyful first step, made possible by everyone who supported us.

Project 02

Panti Asuhan Trytunggal

At Panti Asuhan Trytunggal, Carebridge members spent time getting to know the children, sharing donations and fun activities together.

Dancing became a highlight of the visit: “No joke, we danced a lot.” We’re thankful for the community’s support and look forward to what comes next.
